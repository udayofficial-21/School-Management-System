ROLE GUIDE

Who can do what?

Super Admin

System settings and every school role. Protected from accidental removal when they are the last Super Admin.

Chairman

College-wide summary and oversight reports; no student-record editing.

Principal / Vice Principal

School-wide student operations. Principal manages staff roles; Vice Principal role management is delegated explicitly.

Registrar / College Admin

Admissions, student profiles, registration IDs, and roll allocation.

HOD / Faculty

HOD manages the assigned branch. Faculty has read-only access to the assigned branch roster.

Student

Can view only their own student portal and school records.
